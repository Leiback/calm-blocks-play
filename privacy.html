Privacy policy

Little Plots does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Your game progress and settings are saved only on your own device. If you delete the app, they are deleted with it.

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. "Restore purchase" asks the store whether you bought the Garden path unlock before; nothing else is sent.

The app works fully offline and makes no network requests of its own.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Support

Questions, ideas, or something not working? Write to lei.zhang0630@gmail.com. We read every message.

隐私政策

「小园圃」不收集、不存储、也不分享任何个人信息。没有账号，没有统计分析，没有广告，也没有任何形式的追踪。

游戏进度和设置只保存在你自己的设备上。删除应用后，它们也一起删除。

购买完全由 Apple（App Store）或 Google（Google Play）处理，我们看不到你的付款信息。「恢复购买」只是向商店询问你是否买过花园小径解锁，不会发送别的内容。

应用可以完全离线使用，自身不会发起任何网络请求。

如果本政策有变化，新版本会连同新的日期发布在这里。
